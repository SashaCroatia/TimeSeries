## Period doubling cascades from models and from data
In this project I investigated whether a Gaussian Process regression can be used to reconstruct a system, like the logistic map, from data through asymptotic dynamics in
the orbit diagrams for period doubling cascades.

### Files:
1) CDSComExperiments.ipynb: jupyter notebook introducing chaos in dynamical systems. Demonstrates figures of period doubling cascade diagrams for a logistic map and the chaotic nature of the lorenz formula.

2) EMD-test.ipynb, Experiment1a.npy, Experiment1b.npy: these are just test files. Ignore.

3) GP1DReg-Background.ipynb: jupyter notebook introducing gaussian process regression.

4) GP1DReg-Expmnt1.ipynb & GP1DReg-Expmnt1-Cont.ipynb: jupyternotebooks on the cobweb orbit diagram of a Gaussian Process Regression (GPR) fit of a 1D map and the hausdorff distance plot between the cascade diagrams of the GPR fit of the map and the actual map.

5) GP1DReg-Expmnt2.ipynb: jupyter notebook on the bifurcation diagrams arising from GPR fit of the logistic map.

6) GP1DReg-Expmnt3.ipynb: jupyter notebook demonstrating iterated versions of the GPR fit of the logistic map.
